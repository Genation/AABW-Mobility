Hackathon Product Pitch

RouteMate — search the journey, not just the destination.

An intelligent, route-aware discovery experience built for our self-drive rental customers.

Ho Chi Minh City Phan Thiết
The Problem

Maps find destinations. Not complete journeys.

Driving from Ho Chi Minh City to Phan Thiết, a map shows you the road — but you're still on your own to search for a hotel, a restaurant, a petrol station, or an EV charger, and to guess whether the result creates a big detour.

Today

  • You must know what to search for
  • Separate searches for every stop
  • No sense of detour or added time
  • Results ignore vehicle type (EV vs petrol)

With RouteMate

  • RouteMate understands the journey for you
  • One flow recommends every stop you'll need
  • Every result is ranked by detour cost
  • Personalized to rented vehicle & trip length
How It Works

Destination in. Complete journey out.

The LLM reasons about what you'll likely need on this trip. The nearby-search engine finds the real places. Together, they turn a route into a plan — without ever inventing a business that doesn't exist.

Two trips. One experience.

Why It Wins

Better trips for users. A new channel for the business.

USER VALUE

  • Fewer searches and less trip planning
  • Recommendations that fit the whole journey
  • Detour-aware results, every time
  • Personalized by distance and rented vehicle type

BUSINESS VALUE

  • Sponsored placements along relevant routes
  • Hotel and restaurant booking commissions
  • Charging and petrol partnerships
  • A more valuable self-drive rental experience
  • Strong intent signals without intrusive ads
