The Problem

Maps find destinations.
Not complete journeys.

Driving from Ho Chi Minh City to Phan Thiet, a map shows you the road. But you're on your own to search for hotels, restaurants, EV chargers — and guess whether each detour is worth it.

Today — The Pain

  • × You must know exactly what to search for
  • × Separate searches for every stop
  • × No awareness of detour cost
  • × Results ignore vehicle type (EV vs petrol)

With RouteMate

  • ✓ RouteMate understands the journey for you
  • ✓ One seamless flow recommends every stop
  • ✓ Every result ranked by detour cost
  • ✓ Personalized to rented vehicle and trip length
The Solution

Complex queries.
Structured intent. Real results.

When a user types "ks gan bien co tram sac" (hotel near beach with charging station), our Agent analyzes context via ultra-fast NER. The LLM understands intent — the Search Engine returns only verified, real places. Zero hallucination.
